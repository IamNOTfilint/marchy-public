The idea

Your taste. A safe floor.

Marchy keeps the choice in your hands without letting the defaults quietly compromise the system.

01

Okonomi

“As you like it.” Choose your browser, office suite, AI tools, desktop style, and workflow. No pre-selected boxes.

02

Okimari

“The set menu.” Pick a known-good course: Nami, Jo, Tokujo, or Hardened. Previewable and reversible.

03

Safety floor

Firewall on, full-disk encryption, signed packages, no passwordless sudo, no telemetry. Not a taste choice.

What you get

A desktop that explains itself.

Every option says what it changes and how to undo it. The installer is a conversation, not a guessing game.

  • 01

    Choose your own

  • 02

    Presets that respect you

    Okimari courses are starting points, not lock-in.

  • 03

    Safety you can verify

    marchy verify checks the floor instead of asking you to trust a checkbox.
